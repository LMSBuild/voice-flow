Voice Flow

Talk, and it types for you, under your own brand.

Hold a key, say what you want, let go. Your words appear wherever your cursor is. It runs 100% on your Mac: private, offline, no subscription. Works on any Mac, new or old.

Install it (about 5 minutes)

1. Double-click Install Voice Flow.command (in this folder).
If your Mac says it "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify": click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password. It's safe. It just isn't registered with Apple.
2. Make it yours. It asks three quick questions:
• Name: e.g. "Smith Plumbing Flow"
• Logo: pick your logo file (PNG or JPG; square works best), or Skip
• Words: names it should always spell right (your business, team, products, suburbs), or leave blank
3. A Settings window opens. Switch ON your app in the Accessibility list (it turns blue). Enter your Mac password if asked.
4. Your app's window walks you through the rest. If it asks which model to use, pick Parakeet V3. It downloads once, which takes about a minute and needs internet.
5. Click into any text box, hold Option + Space, say a sentence, and let go. If it asks to use the microphone, click Allow, then try again.

How to use it

Anywhere you can type (email, texts, notes, browser), hold Option + Space, talk, then let go. Press Esc to cancel. You can change the key in the app's settings.

Want a different name or logo later? Just run the installer again. Your settings and history are kept.

Built on Handy, the open-source dictation app (MIT licence, github.com/cjpais/Handy).
